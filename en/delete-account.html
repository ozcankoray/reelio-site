Delete your account

In the app

  1. Open Reelio and tap the profile icon at the top right.
  2. Tap Delete account at the bottom and confirm.

Your account, profile and all asset, liability and cash-flow records are deleted immediately and leave system backups within 30 days. This cannot be undone.

Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription. If you have one, cancel it first in iPhone Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions.

If you can't open the app

Email korayozcan33@gmail.com from the address on your account with the subject "Delete account". After verifying it is you, we delete the account within 30 days.
