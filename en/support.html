FAQ

How often are prices updated?

Exchange rates, gold, stock and fund prices update every business day. Each value shows when it was last updated.

How do I cancel my subscription?

iPhone Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → Reelio. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current period and your data is kept.

My Premium purchase is missing.

In the app, tap the profile icon at the top right, then Restore purchases under Subscription, signed in with the same Apple ID.
