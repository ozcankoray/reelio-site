Privacy policy

Last updated 21 October 2026. The Turkish version is the KVKK information notice and prevails if the two differ.

1. Who we are

The data controller is Koray Özcan ("Reelio", "we"). Contact: korayozcan33@gmail.com.

2. What we process

Data Why
Identity and contact: email address; the account identifier Apple or Google gives us when you sign in with them (if you hide your email with Apple, we only see Apple's relay address) To create your account and recognise you
Profile: country, base currency, birth year, language, theme To show amounts in the right currency and against inflation, and for financial-independence calculations
Financial records you enter: assets, liabilities, instalments, income and spending entries, categories, goal and scenario settings, and the daily net worth calculated from them The core service: showing your net worth, real return and cash flow
Subscription: plan, start and end dates, trial status (we never see payment details; Apple takes the payment) To unlock Premium
Crash reports: the error, where in the code it happened, device model and iOS version To find and fix bugs. Anything shaped like an email address or a sign-in token is removed before sending; amounts and asset names are never sent.

We show no ads, do no tracking and never sell your data.

3. Legal bases

  • Account, profile and financial records: necessary to perform our contract with you.
  • Crash reports and security logs: our legitimate interest in keeping the app working and safe.
  • Subscription and billing records: legal obligations.
  • Benchmark: your consent, which you can withdraw at any time in Profile.

5. Benchmark (optional)

If you turn the benchmark on, your net worth only contributes to an aggregate median for your country and age band. A group with fewer than 30 people is never computed. No other user can see your amounts or who you are.

6. Statement import

This feature is optional and the app asks for your separate consent before your first import. The statement file never leaves your phone. Your phone reads the text and, before sending it, masks your name, the names of people you transfer money to, IBAN, card and account numbers, Turkish ID number, phone, email and address. Only the masked text (dates, descriptions, amounts) is sent to Google Cloud Vertex AI (Belgium) for processing; Google does not use it to train models. The rows it reads stay a draft until you confirm them. The sent text and unconfirmed drafts are deleted automatically after about 24 hours; rows you confirm become ordinary records.

7. Retention

We keep your data while your account exists. When you delete your account, the account and all records are deleted immediately and leave system backups within 30 days. Crash reports are deleted within 90 days. Records we must keep by law (if any) are kept for the legal period.

8. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and stored with row-level security, so only you can read your rows. The offline cache on your phone is wiped when you sign out.

9. Your rights

You can ask what we hold about you, have it corrected or deleted, learn who it was shared with, object to purely automated decisions and claim compensation for unlawful processing (KVKK Article 11; GDPR rights where it applies). Write from your registered email to korayozcan33@gmail.com; we answer free of charge within 30 days. You can also delete your account yourself in the app (profile icon → Delete account) or as described here.

10. Children

Reelio is not for children under 13. If you are under 18, use it with a parent's or guardian's consent.

11. Changes

If we change this policy we update the date above and announce important changes in the app.
